About
the Dham

✦

Shri Gopinath Dham is a sacred sanctuary dedicated to the lotus feet of Lord Gopinath — a form of Lord Vishnu — where devotees have gathered for over four decades to seek blessings, knowledge, and inner peace through the timeless practices of Sanatan Dharma.

The Dham offers daily pujas, discourses on the Bhagavad Gita and Bhagavatam, Vedic rituals performed with strict Agamic discipline, and a welcoming community where all seekers are embraced.
